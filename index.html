About LASUSCS

A community built to support, empower and advocate.

Our work is centred on students living with sickle cell disease, while building a wider culture of knowledge, representation and opportunity.

Who we are

The LASU Sickle Cell Society (LASUSCS) is a student-led, faith-based organisation committed to supporting and advocating for students living with sickle cell disease within Lagos State University.

Who we serve

Our primary focus is students living with sickle cell disease within Lagos State University. We remain committed to supporting our members throughout their university journey while maintaining lifelong relationships that continue beyond graduation.

What makes us different

We believe Champions should not only be beneficiaries of advocacy, they should become the voices leading it.

Community & Support

Creating a family where Champions can share experiences, build friendships and support one another.

Research

Developing the next generation of researchers and encouraging evidence-based solutions.

Advocacy

Promoting awareness, reducing stigma and ensuring Champions are represented.

Partnerships

Collaborating with institutions, researchers, NGOs and organisations that share our vision.

Leadership & Empowerment

Equipping Champions with confidence, skills and opportunities to excel.

Our history

Founded with purpose. Growing with people.

2021 — Founded in 2021

Founded in 2021, the society was established to address the widespread lack of awareness and understanding of sickle cell disease within the university community. Inspired by personal experiences and the loss of a fellow warrior, our mission has evolved beyond advocacy to building a community where every Champion is supported, empowered and given the opportunity to thrive.
